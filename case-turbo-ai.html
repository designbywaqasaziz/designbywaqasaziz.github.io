Case Study: Logo Design, Brand Identity

TURBO AI

Speed, made visible. A turbine identity for an AI tool that makes slow work fast.

Project
Turbo Ai identity
Scope
Logo, identity system, applications
System
Turbine mark, steel to blue
Year
2026

DELIVERABLES.

  • Turbine logo mark
  • Color system
  • Typography system
  • App icon and brand applications
  • Presentation board
01 / Concept

CONCEPT.

Pure motion, frozen in a mark.

Turbo Ai is an AI tool built to make slow work fast, so the identity had one job: feel like acceleration before anyone reads a word.

The concept is a turbine at full spin. The blades pull your eye around the circle, the same way a turbo pulls air into an engine.

02 / The Mark

THE MARK.

Every blade is angled for movement. Nothing decorative, everything pulling forward.

The mark is drawn as flat vector blades with hard edges and open black gaps, so it stays sharp and readable from a small app icon to a full presentation screen.

03 / Color

COLOR.

The gradient tells the story: raw machine power becoming intelligence. Steel gray turns into signal blue, the exact moment a machine starts to think.

Carbon#283341 / Primary dark
Steel Gray#65666B / Steel
Turbo Blue#317FAC / Primary blue
Deep Blue#265F84 / Support
04 / Typography

TYPOGRAPHY.

Bold, rounded, direct. The wordmark uses a rounded tech sans that feels machined rather than drawn, and the technology half of the name carries the color.

05 / Applications

APPLICATIONS.

One mark, everywhere. App icon, workwear, print and presentation boards. The turbine reads at every size, which is the real test of an app brand.
