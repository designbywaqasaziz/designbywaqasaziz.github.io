Case Study · Logo Design

YUMI

A hand-drawn bouncy wordmark with a stamped hanko seal and a rising sun dot, for a Japanese food brand concept.

Project
Yumi
Discipline
Logo Design
Deliverables
Logo system, wordmark variations, seal
Year
2026

01 · Overview

THE PROJECT

A logo design exploration for Yumi, a Japanese food brand concept with a playful spirit. The identity had to feel handmade and full of joy, just like the food itself.

The solution is custom lettering rather than a font: bouncy, irregular forms that feel drawn by hand, paired with a red hanko seal stamped beside the mark and finished with a rising sun dot.

02 · Design Approach

STRATEGY FIRST

The concept was built from three visible decisions. The lettering: hand-drawn bouncy letterforms that look tasty on their own. The hanko: a red seal stamped by hand, which makes the brand feel real and authentic. The rising dot: a small red sun rising over the i, a quiet nod to Japan.

A large red sun circle and vertical "Taste of Japan" type complete the presentation, but the logo itself stays compact and memorable.

03 · Logo System

THE MARK

Two lockups cover different moods. A bold geometric sans wordmark handles formal settings, while the playful hand-drawn script is the hero mark. The script's forms bounce and tilt, the dot of the i becomes a red sun, and a small hanko seal carrying the Japanese characters anchors the composition.

The system reads as one brand across moods: confident in print, joyful in motion.

04 · Color

PALETTE

Red does the heavy lifting: one vivid crimson for the sun, the seal, and the rising dot. Soft cream keeps the layout warm and edible, while near-black ink grounds the lettering. The palette is deliberately small, which keeps the mark readable at any size.

05 · Typography

TYPE SYSTEM

Aa

Custom Lettering

Hand-drawn bouncy forms for the hero mark.

Aa

Support Sans

Geometric sans for labels and taglines like "Taste of Japan".

The hero mark is drawn, not typed: irregular baselines and bouncing bowls give it a handmade appetite. A sturdy geometric sans supports it for formal settings and wayfinding type, set straight and confident.
