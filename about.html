About

ABOUT.

Designer first, strategist always.

I am Waqas Aziz, a logo and brand identity designer based in Lahore, Pakistan. Since 2020 I have worked with startups and growing businesses around the world, building identities that are clear, consistent, and built to last.

My process is strategy first. Before any sketching, I study the business, the audience, and the market, so every mark has a reason behind it. A logo should not just look good on day one. It should still make sense in year ten.

Along the way I have entered more than 200 design contests on 99designs. That training ground sharpened my eye for what makes a logo survive contact with the real world: tight briefs, fast iteration, and honest feedback.
