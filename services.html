Services

SERVICES.

Four ways to work together, from a single mark to a complete identity system. Every engagement starts with strategy and ends with files you can actually use.

Service 01

LOGO DESIGN

A logo is the face of your business. This service gives you a mark that is simple, distinctive, and flexible enough to work everywhere, from a favicon to a storefront.

We start with a short brief about your business, audience, and competitors. Then I explore directions, refine the strongest one, and deliver a complete logo package with clear usage notes.

What you get

  • Primary logo, designed for your business
  • Responsive logo variations: stacked, horizontal, and icon
  • Monochrome versions in black and white
  • Vector source files: AI, EPS, and SVG
  • High-resolution PNG and JPG exports
  • Mini usage notes so the logo is used correctly
Details

Best for

Startups and small businesses that need a strong, versatile mark to build on.

Timeline

Confirmed after the brief.

Revisions

Revision rounds are agreed in the project brief before work starts.

Service 02

COMPLETE BRAND IDENTITY

A logo alone does not make a brand. This service builds the full visual system around your mark, so every touchpoint looks and feels like one company.

We define color, typography, and supporting elements first, then apply them across stationery and social media. You receive a brand guidelines document that keeps everything consistent as you grow.

What you get

  • Everything in the Logo Design package
  • Color palette with HEX, RGB, and CMYK values
  • Typography system for print and digital
  • Brand elements and patterns
  • Stationery: business card and letterhead
  • Social media kit
  • Brand guidelines document
Details

Best for

Growing businesses preparing to launch or relaunch with a complete, consistent identity.

Timeline

Confirmed after the brief.

Revisions

Service 03

BRAND GUIDELINES

If you already have a logo, this service turns it into a rulebook. Clear documentation protects your brand from inconsistent use across designers, printers, and platforms.

I audit your existing assets, define the rules for logo use, color, and typography, and package everything into an organized file set your whole team can follow.

What you get

  • Logo usage rules
  • Clear space and minimum sizing
  • Color specifications: HEX, RGB, and CMYK
  • Typography hierarchy
  • Do and do not examples
  • Organized file package
Details

Best for

Businesses with an existing logo that needs clear, enforceable brand rules.
