Case Study · Packaging

SHIPEM

A forward-cutting arrow mark in ignite orange, locked to a deep-navy wordmark, for a parcel delivery concept.

Project
Shipem
Discipline
Packaging
Deliverables
Logo system, packaging concept, color system
Year
2026

01 · Overview

THE PROJECT

A packaging design exploration for Ship'em, a parcel delivery concept. The brand had to do two jobs at once: look fast enough to promise speed, and solid enough to be trusted with cargo.

The identity is built on contrast: a forward-cutting arrow mark in ignite orange, locked to a deep-navy wordmark, with the tagline "Fast. Trusted. Everywhere."

02 · Design Approach

STRATEGY FIRST

The arrow is the idea. It cuts forward like a package in motion, and its orange is the only warm note in a deep-navy system, so the eye finds it instantly. The wordmark is a sturdy geometric sans, and the apostrophe is drawn as a lightning-bolt accent that echoes the arrow's energy.

Small ring devices and clean spacing keep the layout sharp and legible at distance, the way a logistics brand needs to read on the road.

03 · Logo System

THE MARK

The mark is a forward-cutting arrow, sliced from an orange square so it reads as motion even standing still. It pairs with a deep-navy "Ship'em" wordmark whose bolt-shaped apostrophe adds a final spark.

The lockup is compact and high-contrast, built to hold up on vans, boxes, and app icons.

04 · Color

PALETTE

Two colours, one promise: it gets there. Ignite orange brings energy and motion; deep navy brings trust and reliability. A cool light gray ground keeps both colours clean and high-contrast wherever the brand appears.

05 · Typography

TYPE SYSTEM

Aa

Display Sans

Bold wordmark and headlines.

Aa

Text Sans

Taglines and supporting copy.

A bold geometric sans sets the wordmark and tagline with tight, confident spacing. Supporting copy stays in a lighter cut of the same family, keeping the system simple and durable across print and screen.
