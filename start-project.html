Payment

HOW PAYMENT WORKS.

Simple and safe for both sides. No surprises at any step.

01

Brief and Quote

Send the project brief through the form. You receive a clear quote with scope, timeline, and the total price. The quote is free.

02

50% Advance

Work begins with half the payment upfront. This books your slot in my schedule and confirms we are both serious about the project.

03

Design and Revisions

Concepts are designed and refined with your feedback in structured rounds, until you approve the final design.

04

Balance and Final Files

On approval, the remaining 50% is paid, and every final file is delivered: AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, and the full source package.

Payment methods: Payoneer and Wise for international clients. Within Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay, SadaPay, and bank transfer (HBL). Payment details are shared with your quote, never before it.
