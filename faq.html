FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Everything clients usually ask before starting a project or buying a digital product.

I offer four services. Logo Design for a sharp, strategic mark. Complete Brand Identity for a full system built around that mark. Brand Guidelines to keep everything consistent. Brand Applications for the real world touchpoints your brand lives on.

Fill in the Start a Project form with your brief. I reply within 2 business days with questions and a tailored quote. Once we agree on scope and timeline, design begins.

Every project is quoted individually after a short brief. There are no fixed prices. The quote depends on scope, deliverables, and timeline, so you only pay for what the project actually needs.

It depends on the scope. A logo alone moves faster than a full identity system. Timelines are confirmed in your quote after the brief, so you know exactly what to expect before work starts.

You receive vector sources (AI, EPS, SVG) plus high resolution PNG and JPG exports in the color variations agreed in your quote. Identity projects also include a guidelines document covering logo usage, colors, and typography.

Yes. Every project includes structured revision rounds, defined in your quote before work begins. This keeps feedback focused and the project moving.

Full ownership of the final logo transfers to you on final payment, as stated in the Terms of Service. Until then, I retain the rights to the work.
