CARTE BLANCHE

An interlocked CB monogram and a high-contrast serif wordmark, built for a luxury brand concept.

Carte Blanche

01 · Overview

THE PROJECT

A brand identity exploration developed around the name Carte Blanche, French for a blank canvas. The concept takes a luxury direction: a high-contrast Didone-style serif wordmark set against near-black, with an oversized CB monogram as the signature device.

The tagline "Your World Without Limits" is set small and quiet beneath the mark, letting the typography carry the confidence. The system alternates black and cream editorial panels to give the presentation rhythm and contrast.

02 · Design Approach

STRATEGY FIRST

The strategy centered on a single idea: the name promises total creative freedom, so the identity had to feel equally confident. The mark was reduced to one interlocked monogram, C and B locked in a single gesture, designed to scale from a business card to a building facade.

Nothing competes with the monogram. Supporting text stays small, letterspaced, and quiet, so the wordmark and the mark always lead.

03 · Logo System

THE MARK

The mark is a single interlocked monogram: C and B locked in one gesture, two initials in one continuous form. The wordmark is set in a high-contrast Didone-style serif, with supporting labels in wide-tracked capitals.

An oversized version of the monogram acts as a graphic device across layouts, cropped at the edge of the frame for a confident, editorial feel.

04 · Color

PALETTE

The palette is restrained by design. Near-black grounds the identity, ivory carries the wordmark, and soft cream panels open the layout for editorial breathing room. Restraint keeps the luxury signal intact: nothing competes with the monogram.

05 · Typography

TYPE SYSTEM

Aa

Display Serif

High-contrast serif for the wordmark, headlines, and the monogram.

Aa

Support Sans

Letterspaced sans for labels, the tagline, and captions.

The serif does the talking: tall, sharp, and confident. The supporting sans stays small and wide-tracked, giving hierarchy without decoration. The tagline sits beneath the mark in quiet capitals, exactly where a luxury brand earns its calm.
